# SOP: How We Run Meetings

> **Category**: Knowledge Base Standard Operating Procedure  
> **Status**: Verified  
> **Last Audited**: 2026-10-22  
> **Grounding Context**: For effective asynchronous and synchronous 15-minute standup meetings, the sources suggest using video conferencing for synchronous stand-ups to maintain engagement, and having members post updates in a dedicated channel for asynchronous stand-ups. The meetings should last no longer than 15 minutes to encourage concise updates. For synchronous meetings, a hard stop at the end of the allotted time is recommended, and for asynchronous meetings, everyone posts a written update before 11am local time. The sources emphasize the importance of starting and stopping on time, and using a round robin format to ensure everyone gets a chance to speak.
