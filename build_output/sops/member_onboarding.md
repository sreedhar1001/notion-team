# SOP: Team Member Onboarding Checklist

> **Category**: Knowledge Base Standard Operating Procedure  
> **Status**: Verified  
> **Last Audited**: 2026-10-22  
> **Grounding Context**: The sources provide a structured 30-60-90 onboarding plan template for new hires, emphasizing the importance of setting clear objectives aligned with company goals. The plan is divided into four phases: Orient & Learn (Days 1–30), Apply & Contribute (Days 31–60), Perform & Integrate (Days 61–90), and Optimize & Grow (Days 91+). The first phase focuses on understanding the company, role, team, and tools, while the second phase involves first deliverables and independent work. The third phase is about full role performance and team contribution, and the fourth phase targets strategic contribution and development goals. The sources also highlight the importance of establishing SMART goals and regular check-ins to ensure new hires feel confident and on track.

## 1. Purpose & Objective
This document outlines the standard operating procedures for **Team Member Onboarding Checklist** within Autonomous Robotics Club. The goal is to eliminate recurring confusion, enable full async transparency, and maintain a lightweight execution cadence.

## 2. Prerequisites & Permissions
- Confirmed account in team Notion workspace and communication hub.
- Read and accepted the team operating norms.

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions
- [ ] **Step 1**: Review the current deliverable status before initiating any sync or modification.
- [ ] **Step 2**: If proposing a change, reference the parent Project in `🎯 [HQ] Projects`.
- [ ] **Step 3**: Document explicit decisions directly in the page body; avoid leaving decisions in transient chat threads.
- [ ] **Step 4**: Assign direct single owners and due dates for any newly created action items.
